# Collections service

Owner: founder. Decision 0020 is the source. Agents may propose changes by PR.

## What it is

A considerate collections assistant for small businesses, offered separately from free ClientBilling invoicing. The user imports business accounts that owe them money and says how each should be handled. A hosted agent, one per user, then drafts messages and negotiates settlements or payment plans within those limits. Every message waits for the user's approval.

## Hard lines

1. **Business debts only.** The user confirms this on every import. No personal, family, or household debts.
2. **The user's name, the user's accounts.** Messages go from the user's own email, as the user. Never as ClientBilling and never as a collection agency.
3. **No funds.** Payment goes to the user's own pay link. We never collect, hold, or forward money. The compliance boundary applies in full.
4. **Within the user's limits.** The assistant cannot offer below the minimum settlement or beyond the longest plan. Anything outside the limits goes to the user.
5. **Approval on every message** until a later decision changes it.
6. **Always an AI, always a person available.** Every message says an AI assistant is writing for the user, and offers a person.
7. **Disputes go to the user.** A disputed account is paused and the assistant does not negotiate it.
8. **No calls, voice, or SMS.** Email and a web page only.
9. **No contingency fee** until an attorney approves it. Flat subscription by default.

## Voice

Human and considerate. No threats, no false urgency, no legal claims, no action the user has not approved and intends to take.
