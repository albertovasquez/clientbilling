# 0012: The accountant channel is a feature, not an acquisition channel, for now

Status: Accepted 2026-09-18

## Context

Bookkeepers and accountants influence which invoicing tool a small business uses and need exports. The brief asked whether that is a real acquisition path or a checkbox.

## Decision

- CSV export of invoices and payments is P1. A QuickBooks-import-format export is P2. No accountant partner program, directory, or multi-client accountant login until retention among merchants is known.

## Reasons

- Our loop runs through the merchant's Collect moment, not through the accountant's tool choice. Accountants rarely decide who processes the cards.
- Exports satisfy the accountant without building a second product.

## Consequences

- Statements and exports stay simple and file-based.
- Vertical landing kits target merchants, not accounting firms.

## Revisit when

Ten or more merchants cite their accountant as the reason they chose or left the tool.
