# CDG: residual and attribution clarification (draft, unsent)

Status: drafted 2026-09-18 from the founder's research. Sending it is the founder's call and reopens decision 0014 narrowly, as 0021 allows: economics and attribution only, no integration questions. Nothing else changes until a written answer exists; CDG stays modelled at zero recurring revenue.

Why this email and not a bigger one: CDG's public 2021 reseller template (a template, not our executed agreement) describes a 30% split above pass-through costs, monthly calculation with a $25 payout threshold, CDG's discretion to change commission structures, termination on 30 days' notice, and no clean residual tail. Attribution mechanics are not public. Four written answers turn all of that from inference into terms we can model.

---

Subject: Reseller residual and attribution clarification

Hi CDG team,

I have an active reseller/affiliate relationship with CDG Commerce (agent 470) and own ClientBilling.com. I am relaunching ClientBilling as a free invoicing product and want to make sure I model the referral economics correctly before promoting CDG more prominently.

Could you please confirm four points for my current account:

1. Is my current compensation schedule a recurring residual split, a one-time referral bounty, or both? If recurring, what is the current split and the calculation basis?
2. For a merchant I refer who remains active with CDG, how long do I continue receiving the residual, and what happens to that residual if either party later terminates the reseller agreement?
3. How is a merchant attributed to my reseller account today: unique link or code, application source, cookie, manual agent assignment, or another mechanism? Is there an attribution window?
4. The public reseller agreement refers to an "existing customer of VAR". Would a registered ClientBilling.com invoicing customer satisfy that requirement?

I am only trying to confirm economics and attribution at this stage. No integration questions yet.

Thank you,
Alberto Vasquez
ClientBilling.com
